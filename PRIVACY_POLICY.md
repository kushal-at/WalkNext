# Privacy Policy for WalkNxt

**Last Updated:** September 23, 2026

## Overview

WalkNxt is designed with your privacy as the absolute highest priority. 

The core philosophy of this application is **100% Offline Tracking**. This means that all data generated, collected, or used by the app stays entirely on your local device. 

**We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data.**

## Data We Collect (Locally Only)
When you use WalkNxt, the app collects the following data locally on your device:
- **Location Data:** GPS coordinates to map your walking route and calculate distance. This is only collected when you actively start a walk.
- **Physical Activity:** Step counts via your device's hardware step counter sensor.
- **App Preferences:** Your chosen settings (like theme, units, and daily step goals).

## How We Use Your Data
The data collected is used solely to:
- Provide real-time statistics (distance, duration, pace, calories, steps) while you walk.
- Save a history of your past walks for you to review.
- Provide a summary of your step goals and progress.

## Data Sharing and Third Parties
WalkNxt **does not** share, sell, or transmit your data to any third-party services, servers, or advertising networks. Because the app does not have cloud integration or analytics tools installed, it is physically incapable of transmitting your data externally.

## Exporting Your Data
You have full control over your data. You can export your entire walking history to a CSV file at any time via the Settings screen. 

## Data Deletion
You can delete your entire walking history at any time from the Settings screen. Uninstalling the app will also permanently delete all your data, as everything is stored locally.

## Permissions Required
To function correctly, WalkNxt requires the following permissions:
- **Location (Fine & Coarse):** To accurately track your walking route and distance.
- **Physical Activity:** To accurately count your steps using the hardware sensor.
- **Notifications:** To run a foreground service while walking, ensuring the app tracks accurately even when your screen is off.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this file in the open-source repository.
